Rules you can inspect.

Above EMA3 high and RSI14 > 50 → bullish. Below EMA3 low and RSI14 < 50 → bearish. Exit on the opposite EMA band.

Spread mapping: bullish sells ATM PE / buys ATM−500 PE; bearish sells ATM CE / buys ATM+500 CE. Both legs require fresh executable depth.

The 1-minute buy bot follows the sell bot’s EMA3 / RSI14 signals with 16.7-point bricks, buying 200-point ITM calls when bullish and puts when bearish. The fast buy bot uses 8.6-point bricks.
